Mango and Orange Fruits

Nutrition fruit mongo: your new naturally invigorating favorite.
This crave-worthy fruit delivers over 20 vitamins, minerals, antioxidants and feel-good nutrients.
With their vibrant flavor and natural goodness, mangos are a delicious way to support everyday wellness.

Importance and benefits of mango

  1. Rich in vitamins:Contains vitamins A and C, which support good health.
  2. Good for eyesight:Vitamin A helps maintain healthy eyes.
  3. Helps digestion:Fiber helps the digestive system work properly.
  4. Supports immunity:Vitamin C helps the body defend itself against infections.
  5. Provides energy:Its natural sugars provide energy for daily activities.
  6. Supports healthy skin:Its vitamins help maintain healthy skin.

Importance and benefits of orange

An orange is a citrus fruit with orange-colored skin and juicy flesh.
It has a sweet and slightly sour taste and is often eaten fresh or made into juice.
  • Rich in vitamin C:Helps support the immune system.
  • Good for the skin:Vitamin C helps the body produce collagen, which supports healthy skin.
  • Helps digestion:Fiber supports healthy bowel movements.
  • Keeps the body hydrated:Oranges contain a lot of water.
  • Supports heart health:Oranges provide potassium and fiber, which contribute to a healthy diet.
